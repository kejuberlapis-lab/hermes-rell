# Web3 Bounties on Superteam Earn

## When to Use
Use when assisting users with finding, writing, drafting, and submitting zero-capital Web3 bounties on Superteam Earn (`earn.superteam.fun` / `superteam.fun/earn`), particularly content creation, technical X/Twitter deep-dives, video scripts, design briefs, and project roadmaps on Solana.

## 1. Core Platform Mechanics
- **Zero-Capital Earning:** Bounties pay verified USDC / USDG / SOL directly to the user's Solana wallet upon winning without requiring upfront capital.
- **Evaluation Criteria:**
  1. **Accuracy & Technical Depth:** Factual correctness regarding protocol mechanics (e.g. confidential computing, state compression, dual-wallet architectures).
  2. **Originality & Distinct Take:** Juries explicitly disqualify generic AI summaries and template text ("we can tell a template from a take").
  3. **Clarity & Hook:** Engaging opening tweet/post with clear readability for newcomers and native Web3 audiences.
  4. **Strict Compliance:** Exact tagging of required project handles (`@project`, `@sponsor`), inclusion of personal links/tip cards if required, and adhering to strict character/word limits.

## 2. X (Twitter) Deep-Dive Thread Architecture (< 280 Chars per Tweet)
When writing thread submissions for X:
- **Strict Single-Tweet Character Limits (< 280 chars):** Every tweet in the thread draft MUST be verified to be strictly under 280 characters. Never provide a draft that exceeds the 280-character ceiling, which causes red negative-character composer errors in the mobile X app.
- **Tweet 1 (The Hook & Mandatory Tags):** Frame a relatable real-world dilemma or contrast (e.g. TradFi privacy vs Web3 public exposure), present the solution, and tag the mandatory sponsors (`@STEALFxyz`, `@Arcium`, `@moonycoin`, `@flipcash`, `@SuperteamCAN`).
- **Tweet 2 (The Friction / Problem Statement):** Detail the pain point with bullet points for high visual scannability (e.g. public balance leaks, 30% platform cuts, clunky 44-char key tipping).
- **Tweet 3 (The Core Innovation):** Break down the protocol's unique technical architecture (e.g. Shielded Vault vs Spending Account, Zero on-chain link).
- **Tweet 4 (Under the Hood / Technical Layer):** Explain how it works seamlessly (e.g. confidential computing, sub-second Solana finality, zero platform rake).
- **Tweet 5 (Conclusion & Call to Action):** Summarize the philosophical and economic impact, invite the reader to try the product with direct links, and include required personal tip cards/links.

## 3. Form Submission Protocol on Superteam Earn
When guiding the user through the `Submit Now` form on `earn.superteam.fun`:
- **Submission Credits Awareness:** Superteam grants 3 free credits per month per talent profile. Submitting 1 bounty consumes 1 credit.
  - *When credits are exhausted:* Advise user to either complete talent profile details (bio, GitHub, X, LinkedIn) to request dev quota, use an alternative/secondary talent profile, or pivot to gasless off-chain social quests (Zealy, Galxe gasless, Testnet forms) that do not require submission credits.
- **Link to Your Submission:** The live URL of Tweet 1 (the root of the X thread).
- **Tweet Link:** The identical live URL of Tweet 1 (for content/X-post categories, both fields share the same URL).
- **Anything Else? (The Executive Note to the Jury):**
  - Provide a concise, high-signal summary (2–3 sentences) highlighting what the thread covers, the technical concepts explained, and the structural depth of the submission.
  - Example: *"Published a 5-part deep-dive thread breaking down on-chain privacy challenges on Solana, the risks of public ledgers in daily transactions, and how Stealf's dual-wallet architecture powered by Arcium confidential computing solves this without sacrificing speed."*

## 4. Multi-Bounty Scaling & Pipeline Strategy
- To maximize income velocity from zero-capital efforts, batch 2–4 diverse bounty submissions in a single session:
  1. **Technical Content / Deep Dive:** High-reward protocol analysis (e.g. Stealf Privacy Layer, 500 USDC).
  2. **Creator Economy / Product Walkthrough:** Micro-tipping and P2P payments (e.g. Moony Tips with Flipcash, 300 USDC).
  3. **Event / Ecosystem Synthesis:** Regional summit recaps and builder spotlights (e.g. Solana Summit Canada, $10,000 pool).

## 5. Web3 Social Quests & Galxe Gasless Execution
When guiding users through social airdrop quests (Galxe, Zealy, Galxe Starboard):
- **EVM vs Solana Network Distinctions:**
  - Many Galxe quests require an EVM address (`0x...`). In multi-chain wallets like Phantom, educate user that Phantom contains native EVM addresses and they can click "Confirm" or switch active networks without migrating to MetaMask.
- **On-Chain Claim Gas Friction (e.g. Gravity / $G tokens):**
  - Beware of quests requiring on-chain claim gas (even if nominally micro-cents, e.g. 0.0036 G / Rp 0.22). Since exchange withdrawal minimums (Rp 50k–100k) make funding micro-gas unfeasible for zero-capital users, always identify and prioritize **Gasless / Off-Chain** quest tasks over on-chain claim transactions.
- **Mobile In-App WebView Limitations (Telegram & Deep-links):**
  - Phantom's internal browser often blocks 3rd-party OAuth (e.g. Telegram login). Guide user to open the quest platform directly in Chrome/Safari, login via X (Twitter) or Email, and complete social tasks natively.
