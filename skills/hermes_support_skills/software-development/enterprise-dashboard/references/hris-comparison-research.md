# HRIS Commercial Comparison Reference

## Indonesia HRIS Market (2025-2026)

### Top Products
| Product | Target | Pricing | Key Strength |
|---------|--------|---------|-------------|
| Mekari Talenta | SME 20-500 | Rp25-50K/emp/mo | Gartner recognized, auto-regulatory PMK/PP |
| GreatDay HR | Field workforce | Rp25-40K/emp/mo | Face recognition, ASEAN 6 countries |
| Gadjian | UMKM | Rp18K+/emp/mo | Simple payroll, affordable |
| Odoo HR | Global/ERP | Free (community) / ~$24/user (enterprise) | Full ERP integration |

### Features Where Custom HRIS Can Beat Commercial
1. **Procurement & Asset Management** — Most commercial HRIS lack this (only Odoo has it)
2. **OKR Tracking** — Rare in Indonesian HRIS market (Mekari has KPI only)
3. **Cost** — Zero subscription vs Rp18-50K/employee/month
4. **Customizability** — 100% modifiable vs vendor constraints
5. **No vendor lock-in** — Data ownership

### Features Commercial HRIS Have That Custom Lacks
1. Face recognition + GPS attendance (hardware integration)
2. Native mobile apps (React Native/Flutter)
3. Auto regulatory updates (PMK/PP changes)
4. Multi-branch/multi-tenancy
5. Bank integration + e-Bupot + mass disbursement
6. SOC 2 / ISO 27001 compliance
7. SSO/LDAP enterprise auth
8. Audit trail logging

### Pricing Model Potential
Custom HRIS at Rp15K/employee/month (undercut market):
- 100 employees = Rp 1.5M/month (Rp 18M/year)
- 500 employees = Rp 7.5M/month (Rp 90M/year)
