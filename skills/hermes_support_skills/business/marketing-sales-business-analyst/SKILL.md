# Trigger
Gunakan skill ini ketika user meminta bantuan terkait:
- Marketing strategy
- Digital marketing execution
- SEO audit/on-page/off-page/technical
- Sales funnel dan optimasi konversi
- Business analysis (requirement, KPI, proses, forecasting)

# 1) Marketing Fundamentals
1. Tentukan target market (segmentasi: demografi, psikografi, perilaku).
2. Buat ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) dan buyer persona.
3. Rumuskan value proposition + positioning statement.
4. Gunakan STP (Segmentation, Targeting, Positioning).
5. Susun 4P/7P marketing mix.
6. Tentukan objective SMART dan OKR kuartalan.

Output minimum:
- Persona doc
- Positioning statement
- Roadmap channel 90 hari

# 2) Digital Marketing Core
## Channels
- Organic social (IG, TikTok, X, LinkedIn)
- Paid ads (Meta, Google, TikTok Ads)
- Email marketing
- Content marketing
- Influencer/affiliate
- Community & referral

## Framework eksekusi mingguan
1. Riset audience pain points.
2. Rancang content pillars (3-5 pilar).
3. Bangun kalender konten 30 hari.
4. Produksi konten short-form + long-form.
5. Distribusi omnichannel + repurposing.
6. A/B testing hook, CTA, thumbnail, landing page.
7. Review KPI mingguan dan iterasi.

KPI utama:
- Reach, CTR, CPC, CPL, CAC, Conversion Rate, ROAS, LTV

# 3) SEO End-to-End
## Keyword & intent
1. Keyword research: volume, difficulty, intent.
2. Mapping keyword ke funnel (TOFU/MOFU/BOFU).
3. Klaster konten (topic cluster + pillar page).

## On-page SEO
- Title tag, meta description, H1-H3
- URL slug bersih
- Internal linking
- Image alt text
- Schema markup dasar

## Technical SEO
- Indexability & crawlability
- Sitemap.xml, robots.txt
- Core Web Vitals
- Mobile friendliness
- Canonicalization

## Off-page SEO
- Link building white-hat
- Digital PR
- Brand mentions

KPI SEO:
- Organic sessions
- Keyword ranking distribution
- CTR SERP
- Indexed pages
- Backlink quality

# 4) Sales Skillset
1. Prospecting (inbound/outbound).
2. Qualification (BANT/SPIN/MEDDIC dasar).
3. Discovery call dan kebutuhan pelanggan.
4. Proposal value-based, bukan price-based.
5. Handling objection (harga, timing, trust, authority).
6. Closing + follow-up sequence.
7. After-sales: retention, upsell, cross-sell.

Sales funnel metrics:
- Lead → MQL → SQL → Opportunity → Won
- Win rate, sales cycle length, average deal size

# 5) Business Analyst Skillset
1. Problem framing (5 Whys, fishbone).
2. Requirement gathering (stakeholder interview).
3. Dokumentasi kebutuhan (BRD/PRD sederhana).
4. Process mapping (AS-IS / TO-BE).
5. KPI tree dan dashboard definition.
6. Analisis unit economics (revenue, margin, CAC, LTV).
7. Skenario keputusan berbasis data.

Deliverables BA:
- Business requirements
- Process flow
- KPI dashboard spec
- Recommendation memo

# 6) Integrasi Marketing + Sales + BA
Alur kerja terpadu:
1. BA definisikan masalah dan target angka.
2. Marketing jalankan eksperimen channel.
3. Sales beri feedback kualitas lead.
4. BA analisis hasil dan rekomendasi iterasi.
5. Ulangi siklus setiap 2 minggu.

# 7) Template yang dipakai cepat
- Growth Experiment Card:
  - Hypothesis
  - Channel
  - Budget
  - KPI target
  - Result
  - Decision: scale / stop / iterate

- Weekly Review:
  - Top 3 wins
  - Top 3 blockers
  - Metric delta WoW
  - Next-week action

# 8) Pitfalls umum
- Fokus vanity metrics tanpa revenue impact.
- Tidak ada tracking UTM/pixel dari awal.
- Konten banyak tapi CTA lemah.
- SEO tanpa intent mapping.
- Sales script kaku tanpa discovery mendalam.
- Keputusan bisnis tanpa baseline data.

# 9) Verification checklist
Sebelum menyimpulkan strategi, pastikan:
- Tujuan dan KPI jelas + timeframe
- Tracking aktif (analytics, pixel, UTM)
- Ada baseline sebelum eksperimen
- Ada owner per task
- Ada ritme review mingguan
